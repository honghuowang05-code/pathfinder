VISITOR INFORMATION

Welcome to Lama Lama Country

Lama Lama People are the Traditional Owners of lands and waters around Princess Charlotte Bay in eastern Cape York Peninsula. YAC states that visitors can take part in activities including bird watching, fishing, boating, tours and camping.

Source: YAC Home and Visiting pages.

OFFICIAL YAC INFORMATION

Visitor check-in, places and activities

Open YAC Visiting page

Check-in

All visitors should first check in at the Lama Lama Ranger Base at Port Stewart.

The base is attended daily from 8:00 am to 4:30 pm.

Visitors to Silver Plains can also check in at the Homestead.

Published campgrounds

Warokutal (Safari Camp – Port Stewart): non-potable water, toilets, pet friendly, campervans and trailers welcome.

Maramba (Silver Plains Station): bush camping, 4WD access and access to boat ramps.

Published activities

Bird watching, fishing, boating, tours and camping.

Rangers may be able to accommodate specialised small-group tours by prior arrangement.

Official warning

Estuarine crocodiles inhabit all waters in Lama Lama Country.

OFFICIAL VISITOR RULES

Camping requirements and things to keep in mind

  • Camp only in designated areas and obtain a permit from the Lama Lama Ranger Base.
  • Dispose of rubbish at designated areas or take it with you when leaving.
  • Light fires only at designated areas in campgrounds.
  • No firearms, shooting or hunting.
  • Driving on restricted tracks or off-road is prohibited.
  • No access to Burkit, Stainer, Marpa and Pelican Islands.
  • Follow all Marine Park Guidelines, Zonings and Limits.
  • Rangers patrol regularly.
  • Stay away from rehabilitation sites.
  • Turtles nest on beaches — avoid barricaded areas and limit driving.
  • Limit driving on salt pans — they are important environmental areas.
  • Living areas are private property. Please respect this.
Source: YAC Visiting page.
